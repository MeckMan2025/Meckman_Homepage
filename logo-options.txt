MECKMAN LAB — APP ICON OPTIONS
================================

All options use the V4 design palette (black, charcoal, tan #FFF2CC, red #CC0000)
and embody the brand keywords: Focus, Control, Discovery, Clarity in the dark.


Option 1: "The Curiosity Spark"
-------------------------------
A single glowing red ember suspended in pure darkness, casting a warm tan light
outward. Minimal, abstract, immediately recognizable at small sizes. Directly
represents the design system's signature element — "the curiosity spark... rare
and deliberate."


Option 2: "The Dark Lab" ← SELECTED
------------------------------------
A moody, atmospheric view into a dark chamber with a single illuminated
workbench — tools, circuits, a soft warm glow cutting through black space.
Captures the brand essence literally: "stepping into a small, illuminated
chamber within a dark lab." Feels personal and inviting.


Option 3: "The Precision Mark"
------------------------------
A bold geometric "M" monogram rendered like a precision-engineered insignia —
clean edges, metallic tan on black, with one subtle red accent line cutting
through it. Lab badge energy. Says "Meckman Lab" at a glance, even at icon size.
